# Vendor contract review (illustrative example)
1. Legal receives vendor contracts as PDF documents by email.
2. Legal associate reads each contract and extracts the term, renewal date, liability cap and governing law.
3. Legal associate compares the clauses against the company playbook in a spreadsheet.
4. Non-standard clauses are escalated to the legal lead, which adds 3-5 days.
5. Legal lead approves the contract or sends redlines back to the vendor.
6. Legal associate saves the signed contract to SharePoint and updates the contract tracker.
