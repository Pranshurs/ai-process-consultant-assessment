# Recruiting: screening applicants (illustrative example)
1. Candidates apply through the careers web form; resumes land in the ATS.
2. Recruiter reads each resume and screens it against the job requirements.
3. Recruiter emails shortlisted candidates to schedule a phone screen and follows up when they don't reply.
4. Recruiter enters phone-screen notes into the ATS.
5. Hiring manager reviews the notes and decides whether to invite the candidate to interview.
6. Recruiter schedules interviews in the calendar and sends confirmations.
7. Hiring manager approves the offer and HR sends the contract via DocuSign.
