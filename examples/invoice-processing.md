# Accounts payable: supplier invoices (illustrative example)
1. Suppliers send invoices as PDF attachments to the accounts inbox.
2. AP clerk downloads each PDF and reads the supplier, invoice number, amount and due date.
3. AP clerk manually re-enters the invoice into QuickBooks.
4. AP clerk compares the invoice against the purchase order in a spreadsheet; mismatches cause rework.
5. Invoices over $5,000 wait in the finance manager's queue for approval, sometimes for a week.
6. Finance manager approves the payment.
7. AP clerk schedules the payment in the bank portal and files the PDF on the shared drive.
