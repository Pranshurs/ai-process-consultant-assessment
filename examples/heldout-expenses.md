# Expense claims (held-out example)
1. Employees photograph receipts and email them to finance with a short note.
2. Finance associate reads each receipt and types the merchant, date and amount into a spreadsheet.
3. Finance associate checks each claim against the travel policy; missing receipts cause back-and-forth.
4. Claims over $500 wait for approval from the department head.
5. Department head approves or rejects the claim.
6. Finance associate uploads approved claims to the accounting system and schedules the reimbursement payment.
