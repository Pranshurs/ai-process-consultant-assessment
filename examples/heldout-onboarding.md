# Employee onboarding (held-out example)
1. HR receives the signed offer letter and the new hire's personal details by email.
2. HR coordinator manually creates the employee record in Workday and copies the bank details for payroll.
3. HR coordinator emails IT to request a laptop and accounts; IT often takes a week to respond.
4. IT admin creates email, Slack and VPN accounts by hand.
5. Manager reviews the role and decides which system permissions the new hire gets.
6. HR coordinator sends the employee handbook and policy documents and chases signatures until they are returned.
