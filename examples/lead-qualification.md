# Inbound lead qualification (illustrative example)
1. Prospects submit the contact form on the website.
2. Sales rep reads each form submission and looks up the company on LinkedIn.
3. Sales rep scores the lead as hot, warm or cold based on company size and the message.
4. Sales rep manually creates the lead in HubSpot and copies the form fields.
5. Hot leads are assigned to an account executive in Slack; warm leads wait for the weekly review.
6. Account executive follows up by email within 2 days.
7. Discounts above 15% need approval from the sales manager.
